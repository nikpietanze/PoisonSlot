Rogue poisons right on your weapon slots, with a reminder you can click when one runs out.

## Features

- **Vials on your weapon slots.** Click one, pick a poison, done.
- **A favorite per hand.** Star it once. Right-click the vial to reapply it.
- **Clickable reminders.** Shows up when a poison's missing, counts down the last 10 seconds. Click to reapply, right-click to snooze, Shift-right-click to silence it until you poison that weapon again.
- **Edit Mode support.** Move and resize the reminders like any Blizzard frame.
- **Toggle anything.** Every feature can be turned off.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/pslot` | Settings |
| `/pslot preview` | Preview the reminders |
| `/pslot move` | Move the reminders |
| `/pslot reset` | Reset their position |
| `/pslot status` | Info for bug reports |

## Good to know

- WoW locks buttons in combat, so a reminder that appears mid-fight becomes clickable after combat.
- Out of your favorite's rank? It uses the best rank of the same poison you have.
